Flexible Engagement Models

Every product and business stage is different. At 1nestep, we offer multiple engagement models so startups, SaaS companies, and enterprises can choose a collaboration style that fits their goals, timelines, and budgets.

Whether you need a clearly defined MVP, a long-term development partner, or a flexible team to evolve your product, our engagement models are designed to scale with your business.

Fixed Price Model

Best suited for projects with well-defined requirements and a clear scope of work. We analyze your needs upfront and deliver the project within an agreed timeline and budget.

  • Ideal for MVPs & small-to-medium projects
  • Clearly defined scope, milestones & deliverables
  • Predictable budget with no hidden costs
  • Structured project planning & execution

Dedicated Development Team

A long-term engagement where a dedicated team works exclusively on your product. This model offers maximum flexibility, faster iteration, and deep understanding of your business.

  • Dedicated developers, designers & QA engineers
  • Monthly or quarterly engagement
  • Direct communication & agile workflows
  • Perfect for startups & SaaS products

Time & Material Model

Designed for evolving projects where requirements may change over time. You pay only for the actual development effort invested, with full transparency.

  • Flexible scope with continuous improvements
  • Transparent hourly or monthly billing
  • Ideal for ongoing product development
  • Quick adaptation to market feedback

Startup Partnership Model

Built specifically for early-stage founders looking for a strong technical partner. We collaborate closely to build, iterate, and scale products with reduced upfront costs.

  • Lower initial investment
  • Flexible delivery roadmap
  • Long-term product & technology partnership
  • Ideal for pre-seed & early-stage startups
